Retail Sales Performance Dashboard – BUSINESS PROBLEM 
Business Problem
Retail companies often operate in multiple stores and towns with different managers and product categories. However, management faces several challenges:
• Difficulty tracking sales performance across different stores
• Lack of visibility into which towns generate the most revenue
• No clear comparison of manager performance
• Hard to identify high-performing or low-performing product categories
• Difficulty analysing sales trends across months and years
Because the data is stored in large tables, it becomes time-consuming to manually analyse patterns and make decisions.
________________________________________
Dashboard Solution
To solve this problem, a dynamic and interactive sales dashboard was developed using Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.
The dashboard enables management to easily explore sales data from 2001 to 2028 across multiple business dimensions.
Key features of the solution:
• Line Chart (Year / Month) – helps track long-term sales trends and seasonal patterns
• Pie Chart (Category) – shows contribution of each product category to total sales
• Bar Chart (Manager) – compares performance of store managers
• Column Chart (Store) – identifies top and low performing stores
• Column Chart (Town) – analyses geographic sales performance
Interactive slicers allow users to filter data by:
• Year
• Month
• Store
• Manager
This allows management to quickly drill down into specific sales segments.
________________________________________
How the Dashboard Solves the Business Problem
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into clear visual insights.
It helps businesses to:
• Quickly identify top performing stores and managers
• Detect high demand product categories
• Analyse sales growth and seasonal patterns
• Compare sales performance across towns
• Identify performance gaps in stores or locations
This reduces the time required for manual analysis and enables faster data-driven decisions.
________________________________________
Final Outcome
The final dashboard provides a centralized sales monitoring system that helps business leaders easily explore and analyse sales performance from 2001 to 2028.
